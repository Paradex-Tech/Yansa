Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how Yansa Energy Solutions (“Yansa”, “we”, “our”, “us”) handles the information you share through this website.

1. What We Collect

We collect only what you type into our forms:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, facility or company, and message
  • Brochure request: your name, organisation, work email and phone number

We also note which page of the site the form was sent from. This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How We Use It

We use your information to reply to your enquiry, send you the brochure you asked for, and follow up about a power quality diagnosis. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

3. Where It Is Stored

Form submissions are stored in a Google Sheet and sent by email to our team, using Google services. Only the Yansa team and the agency that maintains this website can access them.

4. Google Maps

Our Contact page shows an embedded Google Map. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep your information for as long as we need it to respond to you and for our normal business records, after which it is deleted.

6. Your Choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you have shared with us at any time by writing to info@yansa.in.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

8. Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact Yansa Energy Solutions at info@yansa.in.
