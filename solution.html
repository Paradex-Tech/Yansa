What Generates Power Quality Problems

The equipment running your facility is also what stresses your power system.
Knowing which loads generate which problems is where diagnosis begins.

Variable Frequency Drives

Generate harmonic currents that distort the supply waveform and stress connected equipment.

Switched Mode Power Supplies

Draw nonlinear current, introducing high frequency harmonics into the system.

Arc Furnaces

Produce rapid, unpredictable load fluctuations causing voltage flicker and harmonic injection.

Paint Shops

Heavy rectifier based loads generating significant harmonic content across multiple orders.

Heavy Switching Loads

Abrupt load changes that cause voltage sags, swells, and transient disturbances on the network.

The Problems These Loads Create.

01

Harmonics

Distortion in the current or voltage waveform caused by nonlinear loads. Degrades equipment, increases heat, and raises energy consumption.

02

Power Factor

The ratio of real power used to apparent power drawn. When low, your facility pays for energy it never actually uses.

03

Voltage Instability

Fluctuations in supply voltage including sags, swells, and transients. Causes equipment malfunction, process interruption, and accelerated wear.

Active Solutions

Intelligent power electronics that measure, correct, and adapt in real time. Built for facilities where harmonic levels are high and loads are dynamic.

Passive Solutions

Fixed components that reduce harmonics and improve power factor. Best suited for facilities where load variation is low and correction requirements are consistent.

Hybrid Solutions

When active correction alone is oversized and passive correction alone is insufficient, hybrid solutions bridge the gap. Engineered to the facility, not to a standard configuration.
