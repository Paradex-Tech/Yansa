Symptoms precede failure. Always.

Most industrial facilities experience at least one of these. Few connect them to the same root cause.

Increased downtime

Harmonic currents overload protective devices beyond their rated threshold. The breaker trips. The root cause stays.

Equipment failure

Distorted waveforms increase I²R losses in motors and transformers. Heat builds where it should not, and equipment fails early.

Rising kVAh billing

Poor power factor increases reactive power demand. Utilities bill for it whether you notice it or not.

Decreased efficiency of the system

Harmonic losses and resonance stress capacitor banks and cables beyond design limits. Energy is wasted and the whole system runs less efficiently.
