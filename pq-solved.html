Match the fix to the problem.

Every device is a bet on a diagnosis. The right type in the wrong place, or the wrong size, is an expensive way to learn that. This is how the choice usually falls.

01

Low power factor

A detuned APFC is the usual default for industry today. Add an SVG when loads swing fast or run leading.

→ 02

High harmonics

Active harmonic filters cancel distortion as it happens. Detuned filters take the steady base.

→ 03

Fast-changing loads

Welding, cranes and presses need a response within a cycle: thyristor switching or an SVG.

→ 04

Keeping it solved

An annual maintenance contract keeps installed correction performing as designed, year after year.

Active Solutions

Passive Solutions
